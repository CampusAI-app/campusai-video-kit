# Jacob's taste, for video

What he has liked and rejected, and why. Read before every video. Add after every round of
feedback: the date, what he said (his words where possible), and what it means beyond this one video.

## Standing

- **2026-10-09. Borrow over build, for most of it.** Use what the community already made before
  making anything. Building is for adapting what was borrowed, or when nothing exists.
- **2026-10-09. The order is idea, then flow (what each part is hitting), then the point and call to
  action, then build, then feedback.** Do not jump to a finished video.
- **2026-09-16. Never a fake mockup of the product.** He killed a hand-built hero still that invented
  a screen: "Don't ever just create fake mockups like that, because then we can't post it." It is
  about whether the video can be posted at all, not only how it looks.
- **2026-09-16. Redrawing a real screen is fine.** The chat film redrew a real surface from real
  copy and real colours, and he approved it. Inventing is the problem, not redrawing.
- **2026-09-16. Open on the product.** On the Job Match film he preferred the version with "no intro
  to this, just shows a job card." The intro lines were fine; playing them over black was the
  problem. They moved into a caption band under the product.
- **2026-09-16. Rejected:** dimming everything but one part of a card (reads as highlighting he had
  already turned down), and a narrow capture that "looks like mobile and not the web."
- **2026-08-30. Assembly, not authorship.** "You use templates and how the text is written, and you
  use other places and bring them together." Mix proven parts, do not invent from a blank page.
- **2026-08-30. Look on GitHub first.** "There are so many skills for this that you should be
  connected. You are supposed to be connecting all these components together."
- **Smooth and focused.** One subject at a time. The camera arrives early, holds, and releases when
  the moment finishes.
- **Free beats paid,** and the community has usually built it already.
- **Always:** no em dashes, no monospace, no all-caps spaced labels, real logo file only.
- **From his design taste generally:** clean means less empty space, not more. Green reads as AI
  slop for CampusAI. He saves references for their structure and motion, almost never their colours.
