# Before calling a cut finished

Walk this list every time. Do not skip it because the preview looked fine.

## The product and the facts

1. Is every screen the real demo, captured or recorded, and not a mockup?
2. Is every fact on screen true in the demo today? Did you check, or assume?
3. Is the student Maya with her real details, and no real student anywhere in frame?
4. Any invented number, user count, testimonial or school? Remove it.

## The film

5. Is frame zero a real picture, not black? It is the cover.
6. Does every beat have one subject?
7. Does the camera arrive before the thing it frames happens, and release when it finishes rather
   than on a timer?
8. Does anything move for a second reason while something is already moving? Remove it.
9. Is it readable at phone size? Shrink a still to about 430 pixels wide and look.
10. Does it work with the sound off?

## The words

11. Any em dash, monospace, or all-caps spaced label anywhere, including captions? Remove it.
12. Is the logo the placed file?
13. Do captions sit below the product, never over it?
14. Does every caption land on its word?

## In Remotion specifically

15. Is the background at the composition root and nowhere else?
16. Do product beats drop the grain and halve the vignette?
17. Does anything that grows mount children instead of animating a clipped height, and does every
    typed or streamed line sit in a reserved height?
18. Any `Math.random()`? Remove it.
19. Did you render frame-exact stills across the whole film, not one still and not an ffmpeg sample?
20. Did you export with `npm run film`, and did the sound survive (it needs internet at render)?

## Before it goes anywhere

21. Raylight on the free plan, Remotion without a company licence, or a free-tier voice: none of
    those can post. Say so plainly if any apply.
22. Does every beat build toward the point and the call to action? If one does not, cut it.
23. Hand it to Jacob for feedback with what changed, what you are unsure of, and anything left out
    on purpose. Nothing public goes out until he clears it.
