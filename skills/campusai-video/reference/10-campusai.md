# CampusAI, for whoever is making the video

## What it is

CampusAI is a student platform at the University of Maryland, built by students at the Smith School.
It reads one profile (classes, resume, goals, clubs, schedule) and turns it into the next thing
worth doing: jobs that fit with the reason they fit, events worth going to this week, resume
changes, a four-year plan, clubs. University AI is the company and CampusAI is the product.

"Operating system for being a student" is the approved metaphor, because an operating system is the
thing that makes separate programs work together. The point of the product is that the parts are
joined. Your classes inform your job list, your job list informs your week.

**Where it stands (as of early October 2026, confirm before stating any of it on screen):** the
beta opened to Maryland students on September 28, capped at 500 seats and framed as early access.
Never imply the university endorses or runs it. Ask Jacob
for the current call to action before writing an end card.

## Who it is for

Not "students interested in career development." The real starting state:

- **Behind without knowing by how much.** A recruiting timeline nobody published, and the feeling
  that something already started without them.
- **Too many options, none ranked.** Forty clubs, hundreds of postings, six events this week, and
  no way to tell which one matters for what they want.
- **Unsure their effort counts.** They took the class and joined the club, and nothing ever told
  them whether it added up.
- **Tired of being told to be proactive.** They have had the advice. What they have never had is a
  next action that fits their situation. That is the product, and it is the shape of good content.

Year changes everything. A first-year needs orientation without condescension. A second-year is
starting to feel the timeline and is the easiest to speak to. A third-year wants specific and
tactical. A fourth-year does not want a motivational post. If a video is not working, ask which
year it was made for. Content for "students" lands on nobody.

## How it talks

**Show the gap before the bridge.** Land the feeling first (four tabs open, a deadline you half
know about, no idea if Wednesday's event is worth it), then show the product answering it.

**Specific beats general, every time.** A real course code, a real role, a real club. Not
"personalised recommendations" but "BMGT443 on your transcript is why this equity research
internship is on your list." If a sentence could describe three other products, it is not about
ours.

**Peer, calm, honest.** A sharp friend who figured out the system, never the career centre and
never a brochure. Calm on purpose: the audience is anxious about exactly this, so we never sell
with "you're already behind," even though it would work. No hype, no guarantees. Confidence comes
from specifics, not adjectives.

**Show the result, not the mechanism.** "AI" is in the name and does not need to be in the copy.
"Here's why this fits you" beats "AI-powered."

Openings that work: the specific dead end ("Your transcript knows you took Applied Equity Analysis.
Your job board doesn't.") and the question nobody can answer ("Is Wednesday's recruiting night
worth going to? Nothing on campus can tell you."). One that does not: the feature announcement
("Introducing job matching.").

Shapes that work for video: one question and one answer (best for short video), one student and
one week (day in the life), and the two-directional demo (a course planned around a job, then that
course landing on the resume as evidence).

## Generated-looking work costs more here than anywhere

Students spot AI-made content and skip it. For a company with AI in its name, generated-looking
video reads as evidence the product is thin too. That is why the real product is always on screen,
and why the lines in `SKILL.md` exist: em dashes, monospace, spaced all-caps labels and uniform
card-and-pill layouts are the specific tells.

## The lines, once more

No invented numbers, users, testimonials or schools. Demo screens only, never a real student. No
other university's name or logo. Career services is extended, never replaced. Everything public is
cleared by Jacob first.
