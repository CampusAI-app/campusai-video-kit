# CampusAI video work

This folder is where CampusAI videos get made. The method lives in the `campusai-video` skill. Load
it for any video, film, reel, animation, motion graphic or hero still, and read its `SKILL.md`
before the first one.

## How to work here

- **Idea, flow, point, then build.** The idea first. Then the flow: how the video moves and what
  each part is hitting. Then the point: what the viewer takes away and the call to action. Agree
  each before moving on. Then build, then get feedback and change it.
- **Start from what we already know, and add to it.** Read `learning/` before every video (taste,
  lessons, past videos, the shelf of tools). Write to it after every round of feedback. When you hit
  a cost or a limit, or are about to build something from scratch, look for a better or free option
  first and record what you find. See `skills/campusai-video/reference/11-getting-better.md`.
- **The real product, always. Screenshots and footage come from the demos:**
  https://campusai-demo.vercel.app (or https://campusai-stage.vercel.app, the same build), recorded
  with Cap or captured with `studio/scripts/capture-demo.mjs`. Never draw or invent a CampusAI screen. A
  mockup makes the video unpostable.
- **Borrow over build, for most of it.** Templates, parts, tools and techniques almost always exist
  already. Find them first (`learning/shelf.md`, then GitHub, then the connectors). Build only to adapt
  what you borrowed to CampusAI, or when nothing exists. Motion from Raylight's beat sheets, words and
  colour from the product.
- **Raylight first** for most videos. **Remotion** (`studio/`) when the film needs exact control,
  several aspect ratios from one script, or streaming text.
- **Look at your work.** Render stills or filmstrips after each beat. Check frame zero, check it at
  phone size, and walk `skills/campusai-video/reference/09-before-its-finished.md` before calling anything done.
- **Spend Raylight calls carefully.** The free plan allows 50 a week. Save Raylight's design guide
  to `learning/raylight-guide/` once and reread it from there, batch edits, and plan in chat first.

## The lines that do not move

- No em dashes in any on-screen text, caption or script.
- No monospace type. No all-caps, wide-spaced labels.
- The logo is the placed file in `studio/public/brand/`, never typed.
- No invented numbers, users, testimonials, students or schools.
- Demo screens only. Never a real student's account.
- Captions sit below the product, never over live UI.
- Free Raylight exports carry a badge, Remotion needs a company licence for CampusAI, and free-tier
  AI voice is non-commercial. Anything meant to post gets checked against those first.
- Everything public is cleared by Jacob before it posts.

## How to talk to me

I am new to some of this. Explain in plain words, tell me what you are about to do before a long
step, and when you hand something over, say what changed, what you are unsure of, and what you left
out on purpose.
